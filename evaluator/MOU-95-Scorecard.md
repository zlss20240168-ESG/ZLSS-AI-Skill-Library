# ZLSS MOU 95-Point Evaluator

Score each category before release.

## 1. Deal Architecture — 20 points
- Is the transaction type correctly identified?
- Are both parties' contributions clear?
- Does the wording match the actual commercial relationship?

## 2. Commercial Logic — 20 points
- Is the value proposition obvious?
- Are unit economics understandable?
- Are development budget, revenue, and profit correctly separated?

## 3. Information Discipline — 15 points
- Is proprietary know-how protected?
- Is the disclosure level appropriate to the stage?

## 4. MOU Fit — 15 points
- Is the document concise?
- Does it avoid final-contract overload?
- Are detailed mechanisms deferred appropriately?

## 5. Counterparty Perspective — 15 points
- Would the counterparty understand why cooperation is attractive?
- Does the draft reduce unnecessary fear?
- Does it answer the likely business questions?

## 6. Evidence and Assumptions — 10 points
- Are forecasts clearly marked as planning assumptions?
- Are material claims supported by credible evidence where required?

## 7. Next Action — 5 points
- Is the next step clear, practical, and easy to execute?

## Release Rule
95–100: Candidate for release
90–94: Revise before release
<90: Redesign required

A 95+ score is necessary but not sufficient. The final draft must also pass human review.

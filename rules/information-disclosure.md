# Information Disclosure Stage-Gates

## Gate 1 — Before MOU
Share only what is necessary to establish:
- opportunity
- capability
- commercial logic
- basic project fit

Protect proprietary know-how.

## Gate 2 — After MOU / Before NDA
Counterparty factual data may be requested where appropriate.
ZLSS technical know-how remains protected.

## Gate 3 — After NDA
Technical detail may be shared on a need-to-know basis.

## Gate 4 — Formal Agreement / Execution
Detailed commercial, technical, funding, risk, governance, and legal mechanisms may be documented.

## Core Principle
Prove that we can do it before explaining exactly how we do it.

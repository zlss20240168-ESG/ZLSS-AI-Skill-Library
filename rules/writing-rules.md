# MOU Writing Rules

1. Commercial clarity before legal density.
2. Positive language before defensive language.
3. Short clauses.
4. One commercial idea per clause.
5. Planning assumptions must be labeled as assumptions.
6. Avoid unnecessary guarantees.
7. Avoid unnecessary disclaimers.
8. Put detailed mechanisms into later agreements.
9. Make contributions of both parties visible.
10. End with a clear next step.

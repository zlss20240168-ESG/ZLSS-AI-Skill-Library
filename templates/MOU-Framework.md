# ZLSS MOU Framework

This is a framework, not a fixed template.

## 1. Parties
Identify each party and its role.

## 2. Cooperation Purpose
State clearly what both sides intend to develop together.

## 3. Cooperation Principles
Define the high-level basis of cooperation.

## 4. Party A Contributions
Land / market / funding / facilities / local coordination / other.

## 5. Party B Contributions
Know-how / technology / international resources / development capability / market access / other.

## 6. Commercial Logic
Explain:
- what remains with the existing business
- what new value is created
- what planning assumptions are being used

## 7. Work and Development Approach
Use stage-based wording.
Avoid unnecessary technical detail.

## 8. Future Commercial Arrangements
Defer detailed:
- ownership
- sales rights
- revenue distribution
- funding recovery
- transaction mechanics
to the formal agreement where appropriate.

## 9. Confidentiality and IP
Protect existing IP and define treatment of project-generated results at a high level.

## 10. Next Stage
State the immediate next action.

## Final Test
Can a business decision-maker understand the deal in under five minutes?
If not, simplify.

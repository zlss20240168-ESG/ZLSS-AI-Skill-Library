# Decision Log — CASE-001

## D-001
Decision:
Treat the project as joint carbon-asset development rather than a simple consulting engagement.

Reason:
The parties contribute different strategic resources and jointly create a new asset capability.

## D-002
Decision:
Use US$10/ha as the primary development-budget unit.

Reason:
It is scalable, understandable, and tied directly to project area.

## D-003
Decision:
Describe US$30M/year as potential annual carbon-credit sales revenue, not profit.

Reason:
Costs, verification, operations, funding recovery, and distribution remain separate.

## D-004
Decision:
Protect technical know-how before NDA.

Reason:
Capability must be demonstrated without transferring implementation intelligence for free.

## D-005
Decision:
Freeze MOU V2.0 as the current candidate signing architecture instead of continuously adding detail.

Reason:
Further technical, financial, policy, and legal detail belongs in later-stage documents.

## D-006
Decision:
Use verified national-policy alignment as supporting context but never imply guaranteed government support.

Reason:
Policy alignment can improve strategic relevance, but government recognition and support require separate factual confirmation.

# Generalized Rules From CASE-001

1. Identify the transaction architecture before drafting.
2. Never assume a large payment is a service fee.
3. Convert large project economics into a natural unit when possible.
4. Distinguish development budget, gross revenue, and profit.
5. Keep proprietary implementation detail behind the correct disclosure gate.
6. Use MOU language to enable cooperation, not to litigate future disputes in advance.
7. Use conservative planning assumptions and label them clearly.
8. A strong MOU explains value creation in a few sentences.
9. Every MOU should have a clear next step.
10. Freeze a good MOU when its job is complete; do not keep adding material that belongs elsewhere.
